Frame check

BM · Body Dimension Drawings

Check the new frame against Toyota's drawing before anything is bolted to it. Open these PDFs next to this page: BM0240U@2007 › Vehicle Exterior › Body Dimension Drawings › Frame Dimension-Upper Face (Two-Dimensional Distance) and the two Three-Dimensional Distance pages. The drawings show which points each dimension connects. The page for the lower face 2-D view didn't download (a broken link on TIS).

How Toyota measures

  • Use a tracking gauge (tram gauge) with no play in the body, plate or pointers. Use a tape only where a gauge won't reach, and keep it free of twists.
  • Left and right pointer heights must be equal. Calibrate before measuring and after changing pointer height. Seat the pointers in the holes.
  • 3-D distance: straight line, center to center. 2-D distance: horizontal fore/aft, plus height from the imaginary standard line.
  • Imaginary standard line: 300 mm (11.81 in) below the frame's upper face at its center.
  • Where only one dimension is shown, left and right are the same.

Height check (upper face)

The drawing prints a value under each point (A 371, B 608, C 232, D 300, E 300, F 259, G 371 mm). Going by Toyota's measuring method, these are heights above the imaginary standard line. Confirm on the drawing before relying on them. With the frame level on stands, measure from the floor to each hole and enter the numbers in mm. The page compares every point to the D standard hole on the same side, so the floor height itself cancels out.

Point-to-point log

Log the diagonals and lengths you read off the 3-D drawing, e.g. C to g (crossing X) and C to F (same side). Measure the old frame too before you strip it: it's the best match for how your body actually sat. Most frame shops take a second look at anything more than about 3 mm (1/8 in) off. That's a common rule of thumb, not a Toyota spec.

My truck

Keeping it organized

system

Every fastener, connector and line gets tied to a step code. Reassembly is then just working back through this page.

Step codes on everything

One quart zip bag per step. Write the code, location and count on it: "P2-11 · rear LH cab mount · bolt, washer". Put the bag number in the step's Bag # field. The lock nuts go in the trash, since they're single-use.

Two-sided connector flags

Masking-tape flag on both halves with the same number: E-07 ↔ E-07. Prefixes: E electrical, B brake, F fuel/EVAP, C coolant, A A/C, V vacuum/breather, S steering.

Color tape by system

Blue electrical · Red brake and fuel · Yellow coolant and A/C · Green vacuum and breathers · Purple steering.

Matchmarks, not memory

The manual says to matchmark the steering yoke and shafts, the propeller shaft flanges, the front camber and toe cams, and the brake discs to the hubs. Use a paint pen and photograph each one.

Five bins, one shelf

  • Body and interior trim
  • Engine bay
  • Brakes, fuel and EVAP
  • Suspension and steering
  • Drivetrain and exhaust

New-parts bin

Keep every single-use part the manual calls for in one labeled bin: cab mount lock nuts, ball joint nuts and clips, cotter pins, shock and stabilizer link nuts, brake hose clips, gaskets and O-rings. Nothing single-use goes back on.

Photo routine

  • Before touching anything: a wide, a medium and a close shot with the tag visible.
  • After it's disconnected: one more close shot.
  • For routing, a slow video along the rail.
  • File the key shots under the step here so they're next to its checkbox.

Torque log

When you torque something, write the value and date in that step's notes, e.g. "Cab mounts 45 N·m, 10/14". Then you know it's done, and what to retorque at 500 miles.

Tool list

shop

Everything the steps call for. Toyota's special service tools (SST) are listed with the shop tool that does the same job.

Lifting and support

  • 2-post lift with rubber pads and tall adapters, plus 2×6 blocks
  • Body dolly or 4 tall stands
  • Engine hoist with a load leveler. The manual's hangers are 12281-31070 and 12282-31050, with 90119-08177 bolts at 33 N·m.
  • Transmission jack
  • 2 floor jacks, 6+ jack stands, chocks

Hand tools

  • Metric sockets 8–24 mm, deep and shallow
  • 10 mm flare-nut wrench for brake unions (SST 09023-00101)
  • Flare-nut crowfoot for the PS feed and A/T cooler tubes (SST 09023-12701). The manual gives a separate torque for use with the SST.
  • 6 mm hex key to hold stabilizer link studs
  • Torque wrenches: in·lb, 3/8″ ft·lb, 1/2″ to 250 ft·lb (hub nuts are 174 ft·lbf)
  • Snap ring pliers for the transfer shift lever
  • Pry bars, dead-blow, brass drift, punches

Specialty

  • Tie rod end puller (SST 09628-62011)
  • Front drive shaft puller (SST 09520-01010 set)
  • O2 sensor socket (SST 09224-00010)
  • Hose clamp pliers, trim tools, clip pliers
  • Tracking gauge (tram), or a tape and plumb bob
  • Brake pressure bleeder, clear hose, catch bottle
  • Spill-free coolant funnel
  • Thread chaser set
  • Induction heater or torch, cutoff wheel

Diagnostics

  • A Techstream-capable scan tool. It's required for brake master cylinder solenoid bleeding if the reservoir runs empty or a line is opened at the booster. Also useful for the A/T fluid temperature check and VSC calibration.
  • DLC3 jumper wire (SST 09843-18040 equivalent) for the Ts–CG VSC calibration and the TC–CG A/T temperature mode
  • A/C recovery machine, vacuum pump and gauges, or an A/C shop

Fluids (from the manual)

  • Brake and clutch: DOT 3
  • Coolant: Toyota SLLC, 9.8 L A/T or 9.4 L M/T
  • ATF WS (A750F): 3.0 L drain and refill, 10.4 L dry
  • M/T: 75W-90 GL-4 or GL-5, 1.8 L
  • Transfer: VF2A 75W-90 GL-3, 1.0 L; VF4B 75W-90 GL-5, 1.4 L
  • Diffs: GL-5. Front 1.35–1.45 L (1.45–1.55 L with A.D.D., 75W-90). Rear 2.95–3.05 L (2.85–2.95 L with diff lock).
  • A/C: R-134a 570–630 g, ND-OIL8

Consumables

  • Penetrating oil, anti-seize, blue threadlocker, dielectric grease
  • Undercoat for the frame. Toyota specifies PASTAR UWE (V9240-0025) or PASTAR UC for chassis and underbody.
  • Cavity wax with a 360° wand for inside the rails
  • Plug and cap kit, zip bags, 5 tape colors, paint pens
  • Vinyl tape to seal open A/C ports (the manual says to)
